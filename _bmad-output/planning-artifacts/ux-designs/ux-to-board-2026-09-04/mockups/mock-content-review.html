Quick-start Guide

Welcome to Wingspan

In Wingspan, you are a bird enthusiast competing to discover and attract the most remarkable birds to your wildlife preserves. Each of the three habitats on your player mat — forest, grassland, and wetland — supports different bird types and actions.

Your goal is to score the most points across four rounds by collecting birds, laying eggs, gathering food, and activating powerful chain reactions in your engine.

Core loop

On your turn, choose one action: play a bird from your hand, gain food from the dice in the bird feeder, lay eggs on your birds, or draw bird cards. Each action also activates the "when activated" powers of birds already in that habitat, firing from right to left.

Birds cost food and sometimes eggs to play. The further right a bird sits in its habitat, the more eggs it costs — but the more birds you have in that row, the more powers fire when you activate it. Building dense rows is the heart of the engine.

Rounds and end game

The game lasts four rounds of 8, 7, 6, and 5 turns. At the end of each round, players move their action cubes up and fulfill end-of-round goals. After round four, score birds, eggs, food on birds, tucked cards, end-of-round goal tokens, and bonus cards.

Cheat Sheet
Actions
  • Play a bird — pay food cost + eggs for the column. Place bird card in habitat.
  • Gain food — take dice from feeder. Re-roll all if empty. Caching powers fire.
  • Lay eggs — place eggs on birds. Egg limit varies by bird (shown on card).
  • Draw cards — draw from face-up row or blind from deck.
Egg costs by column
  • Column 1 — 0 eggs
  • Column 2 — 1 egg
  • Column 3 — 1 egg
  • Column 4 — 2 eggs
  • Column 5 — 2 eggs
Food types
  • Worm — invertebrate
  • Wheat — seed
  • Mouse — rodent
  • Fish — fish
  • Cherry — fruit
  • Wild (nectar in Oceania) — any food
Scoring
  • 1 pt per bird in your preserves
  • 1 pt per egg on a bird
  • 1 pt per food cached on a bird
  • 1 pt per card tucked under a bird
  • End-of-round goal tokens (1 / 1 / 2 / 3 pts)
  • Points on bonus cards
