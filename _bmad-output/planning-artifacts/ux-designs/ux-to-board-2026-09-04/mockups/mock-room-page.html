Quick-start Guide

In Wingspan, you are a bird enthusiast competing to attract the best birds to your wildlife preserves. Each bird has a habitat preference — forest, grassland, or wetland — and a unique power that activates when you play it.

You have four actions to choose from on your turn: play a bird from your hand into a habitat, gather food from the bird feeder, lay eggs on your birds, or draw bird cards. Each action also activates the brown powers of all birds already in that habitat, from right to left.

Food and eggs are the core currencies. Birds cost food to play and eggs to slot into later positions in a habitat. Managing both is the central puzzle of the game.

Turn structure
  1. Choose an action — pick one of the four action cubes on your player mat and move it to mark your choice.
  2. Activate powers — trigger the brown "when activated" powers of birds in that habitat, starting from the rightmost occupied column.
  3. Take the main action — play a bird, gain food, lay eggs, or draw cards as indicated by the column you chose.
  4. End your turn — pass the first player token if it's the last turn of a round.

Each round has fewer turns than the last: 8, 7, 6, then 5. Use early rounds to build an engine; later rounds reward the birds with the most powerful chain reactions.
